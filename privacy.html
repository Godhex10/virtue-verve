Virtue & Verve ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit our website or place an order, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Details you give us: your name, email address, phone number and delivery address when you order, contact us or join our mailing list.
  • Order details: what you bought, when, and any gift note you added.
  • Payment details: card payments are handled by our payment provider. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Technical details: basic information about your device and how you use the site, such as pages visited.

How we use it

  • To process, deliver and support your order.
  • To reply when you contact us.
  • To send you news and offers, only if you've asked for them. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To keep the website secure and improve how it works.

Who we share it with

We share your information only with the services that help us run the store: our payment provider, our delivery partners and the tools we use to send emails. They may use it only to provide that service. We never sell your personal information.

Cookies & storage

We use your browser's storage to remember what's in your bag and on your wishlist between visits. We may also use cookies to understand how the site is used. You can clear or block these in your browser settings, though your bag will then not be remembered.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as the law requires for tax and accounting purposes. We keep mailing-list details until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where we're not legally required to keep it.

Contact us

For anything about your privacy, email or use our contact page.
