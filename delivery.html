Packing & dispatch

Orders placed before 2pm on a working day are packed the same day. Orders placed later, or on Sundays and public holidays, are packed the next working day.

Delivery times start from the day your order leaves us.

Tracking your order

As soon as your order is on its way we'll send you a message with your tracking details. If anything looks delayed, get in touch with your order number and we'll chase it for you.

Exchanges & returns

We want you to love your bag. If you don't, you can exchange it within days of receiving it.

  • The bag must be unused, with its tags, dust bag and original packaging.
  • Exchange for a different colour, a different bag or store credit.
  • If your bag arrives damaged or faulty, tell us within 48 hours and we'll replace it or refund you in full.

How to exchange

  1. Contact us with your order number and what you'd like instead.
  2. We'll confirm the exchange and send you the return details.
  3. Send the bag back. Once it arrives and we've checked it, your new bag is on its way.

Gift wrapping

Every order is wrapped with a bow at no extra cost. Add a gift note in your bag before checkout and we'll handwrite it and tuck it inside. Prices are never included in the parcel.
